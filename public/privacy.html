SWARMGEDDON

Privacy Policy. Last updated: August 14, 2026

The short version

SWARMGEDDON has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The only data that can ever leave your device is an optional leaderboard entry: a nickname you choose and your run's results, sent only when you submit a score.

What stays on your device

Your scores, per-world records, settings, and unlocks are saved in local storage on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) deletes them.

The optional global leaderboard

If you finish a run while online, the game can submit your result to the global leaderboard: the nickname you typed, your survival time, kills, level, and score, plus the run's world and pilot. Your country (derived from your connection by our hosting provider, not stored as an address) may be shown as a flag next to your entry. Nothing here is linked to your identity; there is no account. If you never set a nickname or stay offline, nothing is sent. To have an entry removed, email the address below with the nickname.

Network use

The game is fully playable offline. The web version downloads the game files from our server when you first load it, and your browser may cache them; that is standard web hosting, and no personal information is collected in the process.

Children

The game has no chat, no user content beyond the self-chosen nickname, and collects nothing linked to anyone's identity, including children's.

Changes

If a future update adds any other online feature, this policy will be updated first, and any data involved will be described here plainly before that feature ships.
