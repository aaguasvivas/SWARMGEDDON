SWARMGEDDON

Privacy Policy. Last updated: August 6, 2026

The short version

SWARMGEDDON collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your scores, per-world records, settings, and unlocks are saved in local storage on your own device. They never leave it. Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) deletes them.

Network use

The game is fully playable offline. The web version downloads the game files from our server when you first load it, and your browser may cache them; that is standard web hosting, and no personal information is collected in the process.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever adds an online feature (for example an optional global leaderboard), this policy will be updated first, and any data involved will be described here plainly before that feature ships.
