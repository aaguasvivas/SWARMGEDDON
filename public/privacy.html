SWARMGEDDON

Privacy Policy. Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version

SWARMGEDDON has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only data that can ever leave your device is an optional leaderboard entry, sent only after you opt in.

What stays on your device

The game saves your runs, records, feats, unlocks, lifetime stats and settings on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) deletes them.

The optional global leaderboard

The leaderboard is off until you turn it on in the game: from the card after a run, from the leaderboard screen, or with POST SCORES in Settings > ACCOUNT. Turning it on asks you to choose a nickname.

When you opt in and finish a run, the game sends:

  • the nickname you chose
  • a random id created on your device when you opted in (it identifies the device's entries, not you)
  • your score and run stats: time, kills, level, XP collected, kill points, bosses defeated, multiplier chain, hits taken, close calls, and whether and when you cleared the world
  • the pilot, the world, your ship paint, the THREAT level, the run's seed and the day
  • the game version and your platform (web, iOS or Android)

The game sends your ranked Daily run once, and a Standard run only when it beats the score you posted that week in that world. It never sends practice runs or runs shorter than 10 seconds. The server uses the close calls to check the run and does not store them.

Our host, Cloudflare, derives your country from the connection; we store only the country code. We do not store your IP address. We keep a salted hash of it, which changes every day, to limit how often one connection can post.

The public board shows your nickname, country, pilot, ship paint, THREAT level, score, time, kills, level and whether the run cleared the world. It never shows the random id or the hash.

Opening the leaderboard screen downloads the public board. If you opted in, that request includes your random id so the board can show your rank. The server stores nothing from these requests.

Removing your entries

REMOVE MY SCORES in Settings > ACCOUNT deletes every entry your device posted and turns posting off. Turning POST SCORES off stops new entries and leaves the existing ones on the board. If you deleted the app before removing your scores, email the address below with your nickname and we will remove them.

The server clears out old entries as players post: Daily entries older than 45 days, and Standard entries older than last week unless they rank in that world's all-time top 2000.

Network use

The game is fully playable offline. The web version downloads the game files from our server when you first load it, and your browser may cache them. That is standard web hosting, and we collect no personal information in the process.

Children

The game has no chat. The only text other players can see is the leaderboard nickname, which the server checks against a blocklist. Nothing the game sends is linked to anyone's identity, including children's.

Changes

If a future update adds any other online feature, we will update this policy first and describe any data involved here before that feature ships.
